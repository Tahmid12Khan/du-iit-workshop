# Cox's Bazar trip plan
- Bus leaves Kallyanpur at 9 pm on Thursday.
- Hotel near Laboni beach, 4 people per room.
- Friday: beach, seafood lunch, Himchari waterfall.
- Saturday: Inani beach in the morning, return bus at night.
- Carry sunscreen, a power bank and a waterproof phone cover.
- Split costs with bKash after the trip.
